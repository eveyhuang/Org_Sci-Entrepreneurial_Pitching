Figure 10. Ranking: the baseline C2p and each family's gain over it, alone and cumulative. The baseline model C2p (the venture, the ask, exposure, the Stage A rank score and the prepared presentation; 44 pairs ranked correctly per 100) in the first row as a black diamond, and below it each family's models at their own level, each with its 95% interval. Of 100 pairs of one funded and one unfunded pitch, how many each model ranks correctly (AUC x 100; the dotted line at 50 is chance), with the gains written as percentage points of pairs. A filled blue circle is the family entering alone (C2p plus that family: C3q, C3a, C4r, C5r; spec amendment 6.9 of 2026-10-25); a hollow vermillion square is the same family entering the cumulative ladder on top of the families before it (C3q, C3, C4, C5). For the questions the two are the same model and are drawn once. The solid vertical line is the baseline's value, so a point's distance from it is the family's gain over the baseline; beside each point, that gain (later minus earlier, paired on the same held-out pitches) and the bootstrap p of the gain. Gains: the sharks' questions alone +10% (p = 0.192); the answers alone +23% (p = 0.002), cumulative +24% (p = 0.006); reactions to the answers alone +15% (p = 0.052), cumulative +26% (p = 0.001); rapport and repair alone +13% (p = 0.070), cumulative +24% (p = 0.005). Sample: 100 pitches with a known outcome, 73 with at least one offer, of 100 analysed. All annotations are model-coded (no human review); the results are exploratory, not confirmatory. Bars are 95% percentile intervals from resampling whole pitches (1999 draws) of the fixed held-out predictions (models are not refitted in a draw, so the intervals understate the uncertainty). How to read it: a family whose bar lies wholly to the right of the solid line adds something to the baseline that this sample can tell from zero. The skill version of this figure is Supplementary Figure S2. The pre-registered verdicts (H1', H8) are in report.md; the one-family models were added after the first run and are reported, not tested. The p-values describe this sample of model-coded videos; they are not confirmatory tests (the pre-registered verdicts are in report.md).
