Figure 1. Model ladder: skill over the base rate of C0 to C5 with C2p and C3q, and the sensitivities. Skill over the base rate, 100 x (1 - log loss / log loss of always predicting the offer rate), of each model of the pre-registered ladder, fitted leaving one pitch out; one row per pitch, outcome any offer. The ladder adds one step at a time: C1 the venture, ask and exposure; C2 the primary Stage A rank score; C2p the prepared presentation; C3q the sharks' questions; C3 the answers; C4 the reactions; C5 rapport and repair. Models: C0 (no features), C1 (control+exposure), C2 (control+exposure+sa_rank_pitch_positivity), C2p (control+exposure+sa_rank_pitch_positivity+prepared), C3q (control+exposure+sa_rank_pitch_positivity+prepared+questions), C3 (control+exposure+sa_rank_pitch_positivity+prepared+questions+answers), C4 (control+exposure+sa_rank_pitch_positivity+prepared+questions+answers+reactions), C5 (control+exposure+sa_rank_pitch_positivity+prepared+questions+answers+reactions+rapport_repair), C3a (control+exposure+sa_rank_pitch_positivity+prepared+answers), C4r (control+exposure+sa_rank_pitch_positivity+prepared+reactions), C5r (control+exposure+sa_rank_pitch_positivity+prepared+rapport_repair). Hollow markers below each filled one are the same model under each sensitivity (point estimates only): both passes averaged (s); exposure as rates per minute (^); without the development videos (D); penalty fixed at 10 (v); every Stage A rank score in C2 (P). Sample: 100 pitches with a known outcome, 73 with at least one offer, of 100 analysed. All annotations are model-coded (no human review); the results are exploratory, not confirmatory. Bars are 95% percentile intervals from resampling whole pitches (1999 draws) of the fixed held-out predictions (models are not refitted in a draw, so the intervals understate the uncertainty). Stars come from the pitch-level permutation test with 500 permutations (the pitch outcomes are shuffled across pitches and the whole leave-one-pitch-out fit is repeated); p = (1 + permutations at least as good) / (1 + 500). * p < .05, ** p < .01, *** p < .001. How to read it: the vertical line at 0 is a model that knows only the offer rate; a point to its right predicts held-out pitches better, and a bar that crosses the line means this sample does not show a difference from the offer rate. The verdicts of H1' (C3 against C2p) and H8 (C3 against C3q) rest on the paired differences in report.md, not on overlapping bars. The p-values describe this sample of model-coded videos; they are not confirmatory tests (the pre-registered verdicts are in report.md).
