Figure 3. ROC curves of the base-rate-adjusted held-out score. ROC curves of every model with features, drawn on the base-rate-adjusted held-out score (the score its AUC ranks; AUCs rank the base-rate-adjusted held-out score logit(p) - logit(b), b the training base rate of the pitch's fold, which reduces the leave-one-pitch-out artefact of a model near the base rate (a residual bias against weak models remains: a weak model can still score below 0.5); they are not comparable with the AUCs of earlier (v3-dev8) analyses), pitches weighted equally. Each model has its own dash pattern and colour, with its AUC and the p-value of that AUC in the legend; the dotted diagonal is chance; 'near-constant' in the legend marks a model whose held-out predictions stay within about 3 points of the offer rate (a diagnostic only; see Figure 2). Sample: 100 pitches with a known outcome, 73 with at least one offer, of 100 analysed. All annotations are model-coded (no human review); the results are exploratory, not confirmatory. The AUC intervals are in Figure 2. Stars come from the pitch-level permutation test with 500 permutations (the pitch outcomes are shuffled across pitches and the whole leave-one-pitch-out fit is repeated); p = (1 + permutations at least as good) / (1 + 500). * p < .05, ** p < .01, *** p < .001. How to read it: a curve above the diagonal ranks pitches with an offer above pitches without at every threshold; the further towards the top-left, the better the ranking. The p-values describe this sample of model-coded videos; they are not confirmatory tests (the pre-registered verdicts are in report.md).
