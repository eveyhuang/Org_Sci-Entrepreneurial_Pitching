Figure 4. Each pitch feature on its own. Each pitch feature on its own: its correlation with any offer, one row per feature, grouped by family and sorted by size within a family; the 20 features with the largest correlation are shown, columns that only count the observations behind a mean are left out. Sample: 100 pitches with a known outcome, 73 with at least one offer, of 100 analysed. All annotations are model-coded (no human review); the results are exploratory, not confirmatory. Bars are 95% percentile intervals from resampling whole pitches (9999 draws). Markers show significance: two-sided pitch-level permutation p-values of the correlation, adjusted for the 77 features ranked with the Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery rate; a filled triangle survives the adjustment (q < 0.05), a hollow triangle has p < 0.05 before adjustment only, a grey circle has neither (5 of 77 features survive). How to read it: right of 0, more of the feature goes with an offer. Each feature is looked at alone and features overlap; a lead here is not a model result. The p-values describe this sample of model-coded videos; they are not confirmatory tests (the pre-registered verdicts are in report.md).
