Figure 5. Substance and checkable support of the answers, by any offer. Answer quality by any offer, from the answers to all sharks' questions before the pitch's cut, pooled per pitch: the mean substance (1 to 5) and the percentage of answers with checkable support, for answers to all questions and to hard questions only (severity 2 to 3); n is the number of pitches with a value (substance / support). Panels A and C: the mean among pitches with an offer (filled blue circle) and without (hollow vermillion square). Panels B and D: the correlation of each measure with any offer. Sample: 100 pitches with a known outcome, 73 with at least one offer, of 100 analysed. All annotations are model-coded (no human review); the results are exploratory, not confirmatory. Bars in B and D are 95% percentile intervals from resampling whole pitches (9999 draws). Markers: two-sided pitch-level permutation p-values, Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted over the 4 rows of this table (filled: q < 0.05; hollow: p < 0.05 before adjustment only; grey: neither). How to read it: right of 0, better answers go with offers. Descriptive, not causal. The p-values describe this sample of model-coded videos; they are not confirmatory tests (the pre-registered verdicts are in report.md).
