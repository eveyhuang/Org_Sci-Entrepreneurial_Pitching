Figure 7. What the panel does after an answer, by checkable support (H4). What the panel does after an answer (H4): the percentage of answers whose first reaction by any shark is a receipt (an accepts turn), another question, or a challenge, for answers that give checkable support (filled blue circle) and answers that give none (hollow vermillion square). Panel A: answers to all questions (863 with support, 682 without); panel B: answers to hard questions, severity 2 to 3 (120 and 190). One unit is an answer and a shark's question it responds to, over the whole encounter of 100 pitches (no cut). Sample: 100 pitches with a known outcome, 73 with at least one offer, of 100 analysed. All annotations are model-coded (no human review); the results are exploratory, not confirmatory. Bars are 95% percentile intervals from resampling whole pitches (1999 draws). The numbers above each pair are the difference, with support minus without, in percentage points, with its interval; its stars are the two-sided bootstrap p-value of the difference (* p < .05, ** p < .01, *** p < .001; six differences, not adjusted). How to read it: when the bars of a pair overlap and the difference has no star, this sample does not show that support changes the panel's reaction. Descriptive counts; they do not say that an answer caused the reaction. The p-values describe this sample of model-coded videos; they are not confirmatory tests (the pre-registered verdicts are in report.md).
