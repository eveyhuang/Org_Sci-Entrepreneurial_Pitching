Figure 8. Coefficients of the full model C5. Coefficients of the full model C5 refitted on all pitches with a known outcome: the 20 largest of 85, grouped by feature family. A coefficient is the change in the log-odds of an offer per one standard deviation of the feature (per level of a category) in a ridge-penalised logistic model whose penalty is fixed at 10, the value the leave-one-pitch-out run chose most often. Sample: 100 pitches with a known outcome, 73 with at least one offer, of 100 analysed. All annotations are model-coded (no human review); the results are exploratory, not confirmatory. Bars are 95% percentile intervals from resampling whole pitches and refitting the model (1999 draws); a hollow triangle has a two-sided bootstrap p below 0.05 (blue pointing right: positive; vermillion pointing left: negative), a grey circle has not; these p-values are not adjusted for the number of coefficients. How to read it: these are conditional associations within one penalised model, not independent effects: each depends on which other features are in the model, the penalty shrinks all of them towards zero, and correlated features share their weight. Compare with Figure 4. The p-values describe this sample of model-coded videos; they are not confirmatory tests (the pre-registered verdicts are in report.md).
