Figure 9. H9: what follows an answer, coefficients with intervals for all and hard questions. H9, the answer-level test: what follows an answer, by the answer's ratings, within pitch. Panel A: answers to all questions; panel B: answers to hard questions (severity 2 or 3). Each nested model has its own marker: support (checkable support and admitted uncertainty, all answers), substance (+ substance, answers with a substance rating), full (+ coherence and context, answers rated on all five; the context rating is Gemini only, not human-coded) and responsiveness (admitted uncertainty and repair before the next turn, all answers). One row of axes per unit: the binary next moves, among them whether the next investor question keeps the promotion/prevention frame of the question answered (log-odds from a conditional logit within pitch), the severity of the next question, and the number of further questions in turn order that keep that frame (each a change in that outcome from pitch fixed effects, on its own scale); within a row panels A and B share the scale. Binary predictors are per 0/1, ratings per standard deviation. For the two outcomes about the frame, the answered question's frame (promotion or prevention) is entered as a covariate and not drawn; answers to a question with neither frame are not in those models. Outcomes read on the model's grouping of turns into exchanges were computed as a sensitivity only and are not drawn (h9_exchange_sensitivity.csv). 118 coefficients are drawn. Not drawn, having no interval: panel A (all questions): 4 too few estimable draws, 1 separated, 3 not estimable; panel B (hard questions): 14 too few estimable draws, 0 separated, 4 not estimable. Every row and its reason is in h9_all.csv / h9_hard.csv (the responsiveness model in h9_responsiveness.csv). Sample: 100 pitches with a known outcome, 73 with at least one offer, of 100 analysed. All annotations are model-coded (no human review); the results are exploratory, not confirmatory. Bars are 95% percentile intervals from resampling whole pitches and refitting (1999 draws); filled and coloured when the interval excludes zero (blue above, vermillion below), hollow grey when it includes zero. How to read it: H9 has no decision rule; a filled marker is a within-pitch association in this sample, not a causal effect, and the rows are not adjusted for their number. The p-values describe this sample of model-coded videos; they are not confirmatory tests (the pre-registered verdicts are in report.md).
