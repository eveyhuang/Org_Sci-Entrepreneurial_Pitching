## Statistics and their definitions

- **Identical share**: the share of units on which the two sides give the same value; for the ask, amounts within 1% and the same equity.
- **Within-one share**: the share of units whose two values are at most one step apart on the scale.
- **Chance identical share, chance within-one share**: the share two raters with these same value distributions would reach answering independently.
- **Krippendorff's α**: 1 minus observed over expected disagreement (nominal: any difference; ordinal: Krippendorff's ordinal distance); how reliably the values are told apart beyond chance.
- **Gwet's AC1**: chance-corrected agreement whose chance term stays small when one value dominates (Gwet 2008).
- **Gwet's AC2**: AC1 for an ordered scale with quadratic weights, crediting near misses (Gwet 2014).
- **Agreement on each value**: for a value v, twice the units both gave v over the two sides' counts of v (specific agreement); for a binary code these are the positive and negative agreement (Cicchetti & Feinstein 1990).
- **Modal value's share**: the share of the two sides' answers, taken together, that are the most common value; also given per side.
- **Prevalence index**: |a − d| / n of the 2×2 table (Byrt, Bishop & Carlin 1993): how uneven the yes/no split is.
- **Bias index**: |b − c| / n of the 2×2 table (Byrt, Bishop & Carlin 1993), with the side that says yes more.
- **Mean difference**: the first side minus the second, on the scale's positions.
- **Spearman's ρ**: the rank correlation of the two sides' values: whether they order the units alike.
- **Same-turn agreement**: both times identify the same turn in the sheet's pinned reference pass. Turn starts are included and ends excluded; missing context, gaps and overlaps are undetermined and excluded from this share's denominator.
- **Share within 5 s and within 30 s**: timing diagnostics: the share of clocks at most 5 s (30 s) apart.
- **Median absolute difference, mean signed difference**: of the clocks, in seconds (first side minus second).
- **Limits of agreement**: the mean signed difference ± 1.96 standard deviations (Bland & Altman 1986).
- **Comparison agreement**: k of n judgements that rank the two clips as the model's rank scores do, with the 95% interval resampling videos (a pair weighted by how often each of its two videos is drawn) and the two-sided exact binomial p against 0.5; the appendix adds the Clopper–Pearson exact interval, which, like the p, treats the judgements as independent and so, since pairs share clips, is usually the narrower; the same counts per third of the pairs ordered by the model's gap; the close ticks.
- **Interval**: the 95% percentile interval of the figure over resamples of whole videos (seeded); none with fewer than 5 videos, or when the figure is undefined in more than 10% of the resamples. The main tables' Agreement and α cells carry one, and in the appendix AC1, AC2, the positive and negative agreement the modal value's share and the clock's share within 30 s; every other figure (without unsure units, per stratum, reweighted, the agreement on each value, the modal share per side, the chance values, the indices, the mean difference, ρ, the other seconds figures) is a point.
- **Band**: the Landis & Koch (1977) name of a value: below 0 poor, 0–0.20 slight, 0.21–0.40 fair, 0.41–0.60 moderate, 0.61–0.80 substantial, 0.81–1.00 almost perfect; the table's column names α's, the prevalence flag AC1's or AC2's. A descriptive name, not a calibrated benchmark.
- **Prevalence flag**: the most common value is at least 75% of the two sides' answers taken together: read AC1 or AC2 (given in the flag with its band) rather than α.
- **Small-n flag**: fewer than 20 units (distinct pitches, question–answer pairs or pairs of clips; a unit two coders judged counts once): the counts are shown; a chance-corrected statistic on this few units is unstable.
